The Legend of Blackwood

Historic Haunts Awakened After Dark

First erected in 1884, the Blackwood Grounds hold century-old mysteries. Once a year when the veil thins, our 40-acre estate transforms into the ultimate nocturnal festival of frights, music, and dark arts.

"Once the iron gates lock behind you, there is only one way through the dark..."

Featuring Hollywood-grade special effects, certified haunt actors, hyper-realistic crypt builds, and synchronized binaural audio design crafted to test the bravest souls.

Upon completing your booking on this site, your ticket pass is generated with a unique reference barcode. You can present the pass on your mobile smartphone screen or print it out at the gate scanners for contactless express entry.
